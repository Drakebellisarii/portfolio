# Product

## Users
Drake Bellisari — a Trinity College CS graduate (May 2026) using this portfolio to land software engineering roles. Visitors are hiring managers, technical recruiters, and senior engineers who scan quickly and trust design quality as a signal of the candidate's craft.

## Product Purpose
A personal developer portfolio that doubles as a design artifact. The site must convey technical depth, creative taste, and professional credibility simultaneously. Design quality IS the product signal.

## Register
brand

## Brand Tone
Confident without being loud. Precise. Slightly editorial. The portfolio of someone who has both shipped production code and cares about how things look.

## Anti-References
- Generic SaaS portfolio templates (icon + heading + card grids)
- Neon-on-black "hacker" aesthetics
- Pastel startup vibes
- Anything that looks like a Tailwind UI copy-paste

## Strategic Principles
- Every section should feel intentional, not assembled from components
- Typography carries the weight — spacing and type hierarchy over decoration
- Dark palette is established site-wide; maintain it
- The education section needs to feel academic and earned, not like a feature grid
